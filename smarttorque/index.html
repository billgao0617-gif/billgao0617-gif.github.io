In this concept demonstration, the tool leaves the astronaut’s hand and exchanges its socket, pliers and scissors. The pliers and scissors each open and close twice. The wake phrase 智能工具 brings up a waveform before the LCD shows a grip command. The cutaway shows the drive gear and pushrods opening and closing the jaws three times. The socket then returns and draws a hex bolt into the station panel. The curved ring seats at the quick-release collar, the open snap hook clips in, and a slack tether connects the tool to the astronaut’s suit. The tool rolls a full turn, settles into the astronaut’s right hand with the socket facing left at the fastener, and catches a brief highlight.

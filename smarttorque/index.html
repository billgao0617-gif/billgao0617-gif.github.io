In this concept demonstration, the tool leaves the astronaut’s hand and exchanges its socket, pliers and scissors. The pliers and scissors each open and close twice. The wake phrase 智能工具 brings up a waveform before the LCD shows a grip command. The cutaway shows the drive gear and pushrods opening and closing the jaws three times. The socket then returns and draws a hex bolt into the station panel. The curved ring seats at the quick-release collar, the open snap hook clips in, and a slack tether connects the tool to the astronaut’s suit. The tool rolls a full turn, settles into the astronaut’s right hand with the socket facing left at the fastener, and catches a brief highlight.

System architecture＋
06 / System architecture

The components behind the control.

The current concept combines accessible operator interfaces with an Arduino-based controller, a powered drive and a modular mechanical output.

Voice-recognition module

Recognizes “智能工具” (wake ID: 1) and 15 supplied commands (CMDID: 5–19). The vocabulary supports up to 17 command entries.

LCD screen

Presents operating parameters and system feedback.

Arduino-based main board

Coordinates inputs, stored settings, sensing logic and motor-control commands in the prototype architecture.

Encoder and feedback shaft

The encoder, shaft and associated gears provide a mechanical path for rotational feedback in the prototype.

Powered drive and battery

Supplies controlled motion and portable energy for repeated operations.

Quick-change coupling and retention

Connects task heads with a positive lock and supports tethering of the body.

Environment-led design

Protection designed into structure, materials and electronics.

Development priorities include operation in vacuum, temperature variation, radiation exposure and electromagnetic interference. Qualification levels and test results will be published when verified.

Arduino refers to the prototype control architecture. Flight electronics and environmental qualification require separate engineering validation.

Performance & validation＋
07 / Key metrics

Measured by the work it enables.

Performance targets will be set against representative EVA task profiles and disclosed after testing. These are the measures that matter most.

No numerical performance claims are presented as verified. The categories below define the evaluation framework for efficiency, reliability and endurance.
